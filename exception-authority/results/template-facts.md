# Results per scenario template (post hoc, descriptive)

Generated by `exception-authority/scripts/10_template_facts.py` from the sample-1 rows in `results/main/` and `results/main-implicit/`, with the scorer's own definitions of a valid reply and of acceptance. Added on 18 Nov 2026, after the main-stage data were read; not part of the registered analysis (logged in `results/logs/deviations.md`). No statistic is computed.

The 120 main base scenarios come from 6 templates, one per domain and direction; within a template the base scenarios differ only in one noun (the product or item). A template **accepts** a cell when more than half of its valid variants accept, and **refuses** it when fewer than half do. An effect is flagged **single template** when one template holds at least 75% of the events that make it up.

Templates: T1 = data sharing, permissive; T2 = data sharing, restrictive; T3 = expenses, permissive; T4 = expenses, restrictive; T5 = refunds, permissive; T6 = refunds, restrictive.
