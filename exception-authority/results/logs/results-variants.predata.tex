\newcommand{\VOneOutcomes}{\paragraph{Pre-registered outcomes.}
Prediction F held for \nFloorBreakClaude{} of the \nClaude{} Claude models in the panel (Haiku: \floorBreakHaiku{}; Opus: \floorBreakOpus{}). For each of them, at least one cell in which the issuer lacks the power (D, R or C-no) had acceptance whose Wilson lower bound, Bonferroni-adjusted over those cells (\floorBreakConf\%), exceeded \floorBreakLo\%: false acceptance was above zero even for Claude models. F is a descriptive threshold per model, not a test across models. Outcome C did not hold (\ceilingHolds{}); the lowest accuracy in any cell was \minAccHaiku\% for Haiku and \minAccOpus\% for Opus. Sonnet, outside the panel, is scored for neither outcome.}

For \fillin{which Claude panel models}, an authentic entry from an issuer without the power was applied often enough to break the pre-registered floor, in \fillin{cell or cells whose bound cleared it}. Spoofing studies report near-zero rates on forged authority; our entries are authentic, so the two findings concern different capabilities and do not conflict.}
